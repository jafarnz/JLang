# Code of conduct

JLang is for people learning, so this community has to be a place where asking a basic question is normal and nobody is made to feel small for not knowing something yet.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners. Everyone was one.
- Explain rather than correct. Assume people are doing their best.
- Criticise ideas and code, not people. Say what would make something better.
- Welcome people of every background, identity and level of experience.
- Give credit, and ask before using someone else's work.

## What isn't acceptable

Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal attacks, sexual content, publishing someone's private information, and deliberately disrupting discussions are not acceptable anywhere the project is: issues, pull requests, discussions, Discord and events.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, message a maintainer privately on Discord, or contact [@jafarnz](https://github.com/jafarnz) through GitHub. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers will respond, and may warn, mute or remove people who break this code, wherever it happens.

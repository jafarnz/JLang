Worst case of each part of the program, at 160 MHz, in the pipeline model of
jlang emu --pipeline. A real chip adds caches, interrupts and its board functions.

the script       at most 134 cycles = 0.84 us, plus printing, jl_hal_putc and jl_hal_pin_mode
on press         at most 916 cycles = 5.72 us, plus printing, jl_hal_putc and jl_hal_pin_write
on tick          no limit: the loop at line 16 can run any number of times (only loops like for i in 0..8 have a known count)
